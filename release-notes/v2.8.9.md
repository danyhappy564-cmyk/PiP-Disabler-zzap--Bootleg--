- 제보자분 게임에서 해결된 것을 확인했습니다.
- 그래도 이상하면 F12 → `3. 스코프별 설정` → `구멍 방식: 화면 픽셀로 지우기`를 끄면 예전 방식(메쉬 자르기)으로 돌아갑니다.

**<Changes (English)>**

**Fixed: the scope's inside showing in the lens when aiming at the sky**
- With v3's default `Pixel Hole` mode, aiming at the **sky** showed the inside of the scope body (a black tube) instead of the sky. Trees, ground and buildings were fine.
- Cause 1: the "far away" depth the mod writes inside the lens circle was closer than the game's sky dome, so the sky was not drawn there. It is now exactly the camera's far plane, like empty sky. (2.8.8)
- Cause 2: after that, the scope's inside still showed faintly through the sky: the G-buffer colour inside the circle still held the scope body, and the sky is blended on top. The circle is now also covered with a black matte surface, so it matches empty sky. (2.8.9)
- Confirmed fixed in game by the reporter.
- If anything still looks wrong, turn off `Pixel Hole` in F12 to go back to the mesh cut.

**Install / update**
- Extract `PiP-Disabler.zip` into your SPT folder and overwrite (`BepInEx/plugins/PiP-Disabler/`). Settings are kept.

If I had spent days pouring my heart into making a mod, only to have someone take my code and modify it however they pleased, I wouldn't feel good about it either.
Therefore, when writing README.md files on GitHub, I will always credit the original creators, And if it's not a mod I built from scratch, I will fully respect the original modder's decisions and opinions.

Please do not do that. As the saying goes, "When in Rome, do as the Romans do"—and showing proper respect and courtesy is essential.
