**<Changes (English)>**

**1. Fixed: the scope's inside showing in the lens when aiming at the sky**
- With v3's default `Pixel Hole` mode, aiming at the sky showed the inside of the scope body instead of the sky. Ground, trees and buildings were fine.
- Cause 1: the "far away" depth written inside the lens circle was closer than the game's sky, so the sky was not drawn there → now exactly the camera's far plane, like empty sky.
- Cause 2: the scope's inside then still showed faintly (the sky is blended on top) → the circle is now also covered with a black matte surface.
- Confirmed fixed in game by the reporter.

**2. Fixed: view shaking and the lens hole drifting with `Keep scope centered with zeroing`**
- Zoomed in and standing still, the view shook slightly (sometimes), and while walking or with breathing sway the hole drifted off the lens. Turning the option off avoided it.
- A measuring log showed the weapon itself was steady; three problems in the mod's own math:
  - The lens hole was placed before the camera was turned to the scope → order fixed.
  - The camera alignment sometimes ran twice per frame with different results → computed once per frame.
  - The optic camera used for the view direction carried over the previous frame's override → the direction now comes from the scope axis and the game's camera only.
- Measured after the fix: at FOV 3.2° the view no longer jumps (max 77 px per frame before → about 1 px, the weapon's own motion). Confirmed in game.
- The 2.7.10 "smooth weapon steps after a zero change" helper was removed (it carried the shaking into the view).

**3. Other**
- With `Debug logging` on, a `[Jitter]` line is written every 2 s while zoomed in (how many px the view, scope and hole jump per frame). Keep it off for normal play.

**Known issues**
- Pixel hole with TAA/DLSS/FSR while turning quickly (edge smear) has not been fully checked.
- If anything looks wrong, turn off `Pixel Hole` in F12 to go back to the mesh cut.

**Install / update**
- Extract `PiP-Disabler.zip` into your SPT folder and overwrite (`BepInEx/plugins/PiP-Disabler/`). Settings are kept.
- Full v3 changes: [v3 release notes](v2.8.7.md).

I'd like to share a few thoughts and important warnings regarding the process of porting mods from version 4.0 to 4.1.

Following the recent "ECOT" mod incident, I've had a lot of time to reflect on my actions.

At the same time, having tinkered with simple mod edits back in the TS/JS (SPT 2.3.1) days, I know firsthand how much time, effort, And care goes into this process.
Therefore, even if a mod is covered under an MIT License, if the original author requests that I delete the source code and stop distribution, I will willingly comply.


**2. Please DO NOT re-distribute or re-upload these modified mods to 3rd/4th party places.**

Even if my ported version has some fixes or improvements, please do not treat it as a long-term solution. Unlike official releases on "THE FORGE," I do not perform thorough debugging or full security scans.
Furthermore, imagine how disruptive it would be if someone mistook my ported build for an "official release" and pestered the original author about bugs in my version. It would be terrible.
Moving forward, I intend to approach any porting work with the utmost respect for original modders.
If I had spent days pouring my heart into making a mod, only to have someone take my code and modify it however they pleased, I wouldn't feel good about it either.
Therefore, when writing README.md files on GitHub, I will always credit the original creators, And if it's not a mod I built from scratch, I will fully respect the original modder's decisions and opinions.


**3. Please respect the original creators.**

If people ignore warning 2. and act as if my port is the "real deal," I would feel immense guilt.
Please do not do that. As the saying goes, "When in Rome, do as the Romans do"—and showing proper respect and courtesy is essential.
Instead, please visit the original creators' comment sections on The Forge to leave warm words of appreciation, encouragement, or even buy them a coffee/leave a tip if you can :)
